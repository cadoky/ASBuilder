Updated for the Shaman changes in game 0.137.0, plus a few things you asked for.

- You can now move builds up and down in the planner's build list. Select a build and use the two arrow buttons above the list. The order stays on your computer.
- New legendary amulet Heart of the Earthbreaker for Rockbiter Weapon. Eye of the Tempest (Stormstrike), Cinderheart Pendant (Fire Nova) and the Rockbiter effect text are updated to the new values.
- Stormbound Echoes now also gives you Stormstrike: a build with that talent gets it added on import, like Rockbiter Weapon with Rockbiter Initiation.
- Legendary amulet effects and amulet stats in the tooltips are now translated too (zh, es, fr, pt, ru, de, ja, tr, ko).
