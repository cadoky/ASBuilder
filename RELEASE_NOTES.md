## ASBuilder 1.3.0

- Every class now comes with an example build that works, so you have something to follow (or copy and tweak) right away. They're added once: your own builds are never touched by an update, and if you change or delete one of the examples it stays that way.
- Rings and trinkets can have alternatives now, like the amulet: 2 main + 2 alternatives each. Wearing an alternative fills the slot just as well.
- Builds have an "Image link" box under the notes for your talents. Addons have no access to talent data, so the only way to share talents with a build is a screenshot link (imgur, prnt.sc, Discord). WoW addons can't open a browser either: click the box, press Ctrl+C and paste the link into your browser to see it. The link also travels with export codes.
