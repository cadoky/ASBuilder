Needs game 0.126.0 or newer for the card-screen changes.

- The selection helper now shows above the level-up, lair and cache cards: your build's spells, its jewelry and a new priority bar with the spells in order (spells to banish last, with a red cross).
- Every box can be dragged anywhere and resized from its bottom-right arrow, and each has its own on/off switch in the planner's top bar. /asb reset puts them back.
- Rounded borders to match the cards, icons at their normal brightness, no more glow.
- The tracker, the planner and tooltips also stay above the cards.
- The tracker's Spell:/Jewelry: lines are now a Priority strip: your spells in order, hover for the tooltip.
- With no build chosen, the tracker lists the spells you took.
- Removed the "Card light" brightness option, the cards stay readable without it.
