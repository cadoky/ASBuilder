This one is for the level-up screen. A few of you said it's hard to know what to pick there, so now the guide shows up on that screen too.

- **Build boxes on the card screen**: when the level-up, lair or cache cards open, two boxes show up at the top. Left: your build's spells, right: your build's jewelry. Only what you still need is shown, things you already took drop out. Each icon has its name under it.
- **Frame colors**: green = spell to take, red = spell to banish, gold = jewelry to take, turquoise = amulet alternative. The banish spells stay until you have all the spells of your build. Mark a spell as banished on the tracker (click it) and it leaves the box too, because the game doesn't tell addons about banishes.
- **Card light**: the card screen darkens everything, so while it's open the game's brightness goes up to 80% and goes back to your own setting when you pick. There's a "Card light" button at the top of the planner: click it to pick Off, 60, 70, 80, 90 or 100%. If your own setting is already brighter, nothing changes.

The game's damage meter button can still sit on top of the jewelry box, addons can't draw over it.
